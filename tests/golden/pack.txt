== docs/CART.md
  docs/CART.md: 16 lines, whole
1	# The cart
2	
3	A cart holds lines of articles (`shop.cart.model`). Its total (`shop.cart.totals`) adds each line's
4	amount after its discount, then takes the coupon's percentage off the rest.
5	
6	## Discounts
7	
8	Two rules, both in `src/shop/prices.py`:
9	
10	- **Bulk**: ten apples or more take 15 % off each apple.
11	- **Coupon**: `WELCOME` takes 5 % off the whole cart, after the bulk discounts.
12	
13	## The page
14	
15	The page (`web.cart.render`) draws one row per line and the total, and applies a coupon typed in the
16	field (`web.cart.events`).
